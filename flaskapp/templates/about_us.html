About Us

An AI-based cloud platform to predict the real-time concentration of aerosols over a region using Google Earth Engine.

This project is under continuous development, and there may be errors or issues. Your feedback and contributions are appreciated. Please report bugs and provide feedback via email at ashutosh.mishra.r5*at*dc.tohoku.ac.jp.

Acknowledgement

Project developed as final showcase for Department of Space Applications Activity, Space Studies Program 2024 organized by International Space University in collaboration with NASA Johnson Space Center at Rice University Houston, Texas, USA.
